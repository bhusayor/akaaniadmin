## Added (second pass)

Sourced via Openverse, filtered to licences that permit commercial use and
modification (CC0 / PDM / BY / BY-SA). NonCommercial and NoDerivatives were
excluded up front — the admin crops these to fixed aspect ratios.

unreliable: a result titled "Moi/Moi" was a photograph of a private
individual, and one titled "black bean fritter" was a plated restaurant
